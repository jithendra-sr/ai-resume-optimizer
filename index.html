Why resumes fail ATS and what helps

Common problems

  • Missing keywords: your wording differs from the job posting.
  • Complex layouts: columns, tables and graphics can confuse parsers.
  • Vague bullets: duties listed instead of results.

What ResumeAI does

  • Keyword gaps: shows which terms from the job are missing.
  • Clean structure: rewrites into a simple single-column format.
  • Fit score: gives an estimate before you apply. It is a guide, not a guarantee.

FAQ

How do free checks work?

Verify your email once and get 3 free checks. Each score or rewrite uses one check, and failed requests are not charged.

Will my resume pass every ATS?

No tool can promise that. ResumeAI improves keyword match and formatting, which are the most common reasons for rejection.

Is my data private?

Your text is sent over HTTPS to our AI provider to produce the result and is not stored by this app. Always review the AI output and fix any details before sending.
