The ATS Rejection Trap

Over 75% of resumes are discarded before a human ever reads them. Automated parsers fail when:

  • • Missing Keywords: Descriptions lack exact semantic terminology required by job filters.
  • • Complex Parsing: Multi-column layouts, graphics, and tables break automated text extractors.
  • • Vague Impact: Bullet points state responsibilities instead of measurable achievements.
